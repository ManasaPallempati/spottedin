# Spotted In brand direction

Product name: **Spotted In**

Brand reference board:
https://www.pinterest.com/manasasai1999/spotted/

Short link supplied by the product owner:
https://pin.it/4vqO0ExdI

## Observed direction

- Powder blue, blush pink, burgundy, cream, muted red, and small mustard accents.
- Vintage stripes, gingham/check grids, narrow borders, and boutique packaging labels.
- Editorial serif display type paired with restrained script accents and clean body text.
- Small stamped/seal-like marks rather than large generic marketplace icons.
- Soft, nostalgic, fashion-editorial tone; avoid the current saturated purple SaaS look.

## Guardrails

- This board is the visual source of truth for the future logo and consolidated theme pass.
- Do not copy a pinned logo or illustration directly. Create an original Spotted In mark
  that uses the board only as mood, palette, typography, and composition reference.
- Do not perform the full reskin until the real backend and device-installable skeleton
  are available for product-owner review.
- Final logo, typefaces, exact colors, and component tokens require visual approval.
